01 — How we work

An engagement built in four stages, not one report.

01

Diagnose

We map current waste flows, tonnage, concession boundaries and stakeholder positions before proposing anything — so recommendations are grounded in what's actually there.

02

Design

Route plans, bin specifications, fleet sizing and facility layouts, sized to the district and to what a client can realistically staff and finance.

03

Navigate

We work through the regulatory path — licensing, municipal permitting, business registration — before capital is committed, not after.

04

Operate

Handover into operation, with staffing plans, billing systems and a first-90-days performance review built in from the start.

02 — What we do

Advisory work across the full waste system.

01

Regulatory & concession strategy

Reading the exclusivity and licensing landscape correctly before a client spends on trucks or bins — including where private, complementary, or pilot models are viable.

02

Collection system design

Sub-sector routing, pickup frequency, and bin specification for solid waste and recyclables, sized to household density and property type.

03

Fleet & facility planning

Vehicle sizing, depot and wash-yard layout, and equipment procurement plans that match actual route volumes rather than generic assumptions.

04

Recycling & materials recovery

Source-separation programs and sorting-point design that keep recoverable material out of landfill without adding cost residents won't pay.

05

Financial & tariff modelling

Subscription pricing, revenue projections, and start-up budgets built line by line — registration, labour, equipment — not rounded estimates.

06

Municipal & PPP advisory

Support for local authorities structuring pilot zones, sub-contracts, or public-private partnerships in sanitation.
